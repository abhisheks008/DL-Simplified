What These Numbers Mean

  • Accuracy: Overall correctness - what % of all predictions were right
  • Precision: Of predicted purchases, how many actually bought (trust the prediction)
  • Recall: Of actual buyers, how many did we correctly identify (catch the buyers)
  • F1-Score: Balance between precision and recall (0-1, higher is better)
  • ROC-AUC: Model's ability to distinguish buyers from non-buyers (0-1, higher is better)

Key Findings

  • Sequential models (LSTM/GRU) beat MLP - page visit order matters for purchase intent
  • Deep Network is 1.5% better than GRU but uses 6x more parameters - trade-off between accuracy and speed
  • All models catch ~65% of actual buyers, with 72-76% precision on predictions
  • ROC-AUC scores (0.867-0.895) show strong ability to distinguish buyers from non-buyers
  • Dataset class imbalance (84.5% non-purchase) handled with stratified split during training
